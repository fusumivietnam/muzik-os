Product Blueprint: [domain requirements](./domain-requirements.md), [business rules](./business-rules.md), [data dictionary](./data-dictionary.md), [synthetic acceptance scenarios](./synthetic-scenarios.md). R0 remains the first execution gate; later requirements are not blanket implementation authorization.
